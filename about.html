WHY WE'RE BUILDING REKODI

It started with a simple question.

What happens to an institution's governance when the people who understand its history are no longer there?

01
THE OBSERVATION

Institutions already do the work of governance.

Meetings happen. Decisions are made. Responsibilities are assigned. Reports are prepared. Policies are approved. Leaders work to move their institutions forward.

Yet much of the knowledge connecting those activities can remain scattered across files, emails, offices, systems and individual memory.

02
THE REALIZATION

The challenge wasn't simply creating more records.

Institutions already create records. The deeper challenge is preserving the relationships between decisions, responsibilities, progress, evidence and the context surrounding them.

Because when those relationships are lost, the next person may inherit documents without inheriting the institutional understanding behind them.

03
THE IDEA

What if governance could carry its own memory?

What if decisions remained connected to what followed? Responsibilities remained visible until they were carried forward? Leadership transitions inherited context instead of fragments?

And what if an audit, inspection or governance review did not begin with searching for evidence — because the evidence had been preserved as part of governance itself?

THIS BECAME REKODI

Not another place to store governance.

A governance environment designed to help institutions remember, continue and demonstrate.

Rekodi is being built around a simple belief: the systems supporting governance should be as dependable as the institutions they are meant to serve.
