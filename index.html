THE GOVERNANCE GAP

Institutions remember less than their people do.

Every meeting leaves decisions. Every committee creates responsibilities. Every leadership team gains knowledge. But when that knowledge lives across people, files and disconnected processes, the institution slowly loses its memory.

WITHOUT INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY

“Why was this decision made?”

The people who knew have already left.
01

Decisions lose their context.

Minutes may show what was agreed, but the reasoning, supporting records and responsibilities behind the decision are often difficult to trace later.

02

Responsibilities become difficult to follow.

Actions agreed in meetings can become disconnected from the people responsible for carrying them forward.

03

Leadership transitions create knowledge gaps.

New leaders inherit positions, but not always the history, commitments and governance context that came with them.

Rekodi gives the institution a memory of its own.
